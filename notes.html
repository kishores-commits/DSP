// 45 lecture hours

Three units,
traversed in order.

Topics are grouped exactly as they're taught. Expand a unit to see its full topic list — treat it as the syllabus checklist while you revise.

I Structures

18 hours
Foundations
  • Algorithm design paradigms, an overview
  • Data structure classification: linear vs. non-linear
  • Python built-ins: lists, tuples, dictionaries, sets
  • Comprehensions and their types; strings and slicing
Arrays
  • Operations, applications, and limitations
  • Implementing lists and pointer-based structures
Singly linked lists
  • Definitions, node types, other node variants
  • Append, size, traversal, delete, search, and clear operations
Doubly & circular linked lists
  • Doubly linked node structure; append, delete, search
  • Circular lists: append, delete, and iteration

II Stacks, Queues & Trees

18 hours
Stacks
  • Implementation, push, pop, and peek
  • Bracket-matching and expression evaluation applications
Queues
  • List-based queues: enqueue / dequeue
  • Stack-based and node-based queue implementations
  • Queue class design and real-world applications
Trees
  • Terminology, tree nodes, and binary trees
  • Binary search trees: implementation and operations
  • Finding min / max, inserting, deleting, searching
  • Traversal: inorder, preorder, postorder (and their notations), plus breadth-first traversal
  • Expression trees, parsing reverse Polish expressions, and balancing trees

III Graphs, Searching & Sorting

9 hours
Graphs
  • Directed, undirected, and weighted graphs
  • Representation: adjacency list vs. adjacency matrix
  • Traversal: DFS and BFS, plus other graph methods
Searching
  • Linear search — ordered and unordered
  • Binary search, and choosing the right algorithm for the data
Sorting
  • Bubble, insertion, and selection sort
  • Quick sort — partitioning and pivot selection
  • Heap sort
